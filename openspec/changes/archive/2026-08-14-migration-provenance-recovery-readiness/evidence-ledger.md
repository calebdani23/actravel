## Evidence rules

- Exact local bytes are hashed with SHA-256; archived provider statement hashes are not local-byte hashes.
- A missing or contradictory source is `unavailable` or `failed`, never inferred.
- No row authorizes DDL, DML, migration execution/history mutation, repair, allocation, placeholder/compensating migration, regeneration, or remote mutation.

## Role/authorization register

Collection, ownership, required authorization, operator sign-off, and decision authority are distinct. Current collection covers only local read-only files. Provider ledger access, approved target inspection, recovery rehearsal, cleanup, packet acceptance, and final approval remain requested/unavailable. Every unavailable source has an explicit `sha256:unavailable`; archived source-artifact hashes cover exact local bytes and are not promoted into remote statement hashes.
