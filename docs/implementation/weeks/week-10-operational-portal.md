# Week 10 — Operational Customer Portal

## Outcome
Make `Mis viajes` useful for the pre-travel customer journey, not just quote acceptance.

## Dependencies
Portal identity, Trip, Travelers/Documents, Reservations, and Payment Plan.

## Primary work
- portal home with next action, dates/countdown, overall Trip state;
- customer-visible reservations/services;
- balance/payment obligations/payments;
- travelers and required data;
- authorized documents;
- structured customer requests;
- portal/staff notifications for relevant events.

## Required context
Volume II Customer Portal; Volume I portal/change rules; Volume III Customer identity, Trip, Finance.

## Suggested Changes
1. `portal-trip-overview`
2. `portal-finance-documents`
3. `customer-request-workflow`

## Completion gate
A customer can follow the trip and know the next required action without seeing supplier cost, internal margin, private notes, or another customer's resources.
